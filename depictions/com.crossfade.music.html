Crossfade

True crossfade between tracks in the stock Music app — one song fades out as the next fades in. Works on your local library (owned files), so no subscription is needed.

Adjustable fade length in Settings → Crossfade.

Notes

  • Local library only. Apple Music streaming tracks are DRM-protected and out of scope — they won't crossfade.
  • Requires ellekit (Dopamine).
  • Tested on an iPhone 8 Plus (A11, arm64) with iOS 16.7 only. A12 and newer devices are untested; please report whether it works on yours.
