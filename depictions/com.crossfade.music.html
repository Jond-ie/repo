Crossfade

True crossfade between tracks in the stock Music app — one song fades out as the next fades in. Works on your local library (owned files), so no subscription is needed.

Adjustable fade length in Settings → Crossfade.

Device Support

A11 and older only for now (iPhone X / 8 and earlier). Earlier builds of this tweak put A12 and newer devices (iPhone XS / XR and later) into safe mode: their A12+ part was built with the wrong format. This update is arm64-only, and it refuses to install on A12+ devices so nobody else is affected.

  • On an A12+ device with it installed? Update or uninstall it in Sileo (safe mode still lets you open Sileo).
  • A12+ support needs a new build toolchain and an A12+ tester. If you can help, open a GitHub issue.

Notes

  • Local library only. Apple Music streaming tracks are DRM-protected and out of scope — they won't crossfade.
  • Requires ellekit (Dopamine).
  • Tested on an iPhone 8 Plus (A11, arm64) with iOS 16.7. A11 and older only for now (see Device Support).
