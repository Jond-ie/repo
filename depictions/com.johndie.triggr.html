Triggr

Activator-style triggers for modern jailbreaks. Pick a trigger, pick what it runs, in Settings → Triggr or the Triggr app. Triggr only changes a button when you assign something to it.

  • Buttons: Home, Lock / Side, Volume, Mute switch and Touch ID, with single, double, triple presses, holds and sequences.
  • Status bar: tap, double tap and hold, also per left / right half, plus swipe left / right.
  • Home Screen: icon flicks (per app too), pinch, spread, two-finger swipes and double tap on empty space.
  • Sensors and events: shake, proximity cover and wave, charger, headphones, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, battery level, an app opened, a notification from an app, a time of day.
  • Actions: system and power actions, 14 switches, media and AirPlay, levels, apps, Shortcuts, URLs, Settings pages, messages, speech, shell commands, menus, and sequences with pauses.
  • Places: Anywhere, Home Screen, In Apps, Lock Screen, or one specific app.

What's New in 1.0.4.1

  • Fixes a crash when opening a trigger's action list (in Settings and in the Triggr app) if no Triggr extension is installed. It hit every install without EQELinker, on Dopamine and RootHide. Thanks to 777qwq for tracking down the cause.

What's New in 1.0.4

  • The Triggr app on your Home Screen, plus trigger search, Recently Used actions and a Test button.
  • iOS 18 and Face ID: the side button's Single / Double / Triple Press work properly, and an assigned Double or Triple Press replaces Wallet or the Accessibility Shortcut. Commands and extension actions run through a small helper on iOS 18.
  • New triggers: status bar left / right halves and swipes, Home Screen gestures, proximity sensor, a notification from an app.
  • In Specific Apps: give one app its own assignments.
  • Options → Status Bar Single Tap (off by default on Face ID iPhones).
  • Extensions: other packages can add actions, like EQELinker for EQE presets.
  • Tidier places (one Assigned row), and picking another option of the only switch picked replaces it.
  • Fixes: status bar taps on the Home Screen on iOS 17+, saved Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth devices in the app.

Notes

  • Rootless jailbreaks with ElleKit (Dopamine), iOS 15–18, arm64 and arm64e.
  • If it ever puts you into safe mode, open Sileo (safe mode still allows it) and uninstall Triggr.
  • Source, docs and feedback: github.com/Jond-ie/triggr. Betas: triggr-beta.
