MMirage

Full signal bars and any carrier name in your status bar, even with no SIM.

Add to SileoAdd to Zebra

Add the repo, then search Mirage.

Handy for iPhones without a SIM. Everything is in Settings → Mirage and changes show at once.

  • One or two SIM lines, stacked like a real dual-SIM iPhone.
  • Your text for each carrier name, and line labels like Primary, Business or Travel.
  • Any network type, from EDGE and 3G to LTE and 5G, and 0 to 4 signal bars.
  • Live Signal: the bars dip now and then, like real reception.
  • Wi-Fi aware: the 4G or 5G label hides while Wi-Fi is on, like on a real phone.

What's New in 1.0.0~beta2

  • iOS 17 support: iOS 17 draws the status bar with a new framework; Mirage now fills that one in too. The lock screen, Home Screen and Control Center all show your lines, labels and live bars.

Works on

  • iOS 16 and 17. Rootless jailbreaks. iOS 15, iOS 18 and RootHide are planned for the next update.
  • Tested on an iPhone 11 Pro Max (iOS 16.2) and an iPhone SE 2nd gen (iOS 17.5.1).
  • If it ever sends your phone to Safe Mode, open Sileo (it still works there) and uninstall it. Then tell me.

Good to know

  • Looks only. Your phone doesn't get service. Calls, texts, emergency calls and mobile data are never touched.
  • Using Podified too? Turn off Podified's Status Bar option, or it hides Mirage's bars.
