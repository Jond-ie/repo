Mirage

Makes the status bar look like it has service: one or two SIM lines with your own carrier names, line labels, network type and signal bars. Great for SIM-less and Wi-Fi-only iPhones. Everything is in Settings → Mirage, and changes apply instantly:

  • Dual SIM: show one line or two stacked lines, like a real dual-SIM iPhone.
  • Carrier names: any text you like for each line.
  • Line labels: Primary, Secondary, Business, Personal, Work, Travel or Data, shown as the letter badge iOS uses for dual SIM.
  • Network type: 1x, GPRS, EDGE, 3G, 4G, LTE, LTE-A, LTE+, 5GE, 5G, 5G+, 5G UW or 5G UC.
  • Signal bars: 0 to 4 per line.
  • Live Signal: the bars dip a bar or two now and then, like real reception. Both lines move on their own, and every app shows the same bars at the same moment.
  • Wi-Fi aware: like a real phone, the 4G/5G label hides while Wi-Fi is connected.

What's New in 1.0.0~beta2

  • iOS 17 support: iOS 17 draws the status bar with a new framework; Mirage now fills that one in too. The lock screen, Home Screen and Control Center all show your lines, labels and live bars.

Device Support

  • iOS 16 and 17. Tested on an iPhone 11 Pro Max (A13) with iOS 16.2 and an iPhone SE (2nd gen, A13) with iOS 17.5.1.
  • Built for arm64 and arm64e (A11 and newer, A12+ included).
  • iOS 18 isn't supported yet.
  • If it ever puts you into safe mode: open Sileo (safe mode still allows it), uninstall it and please report back.

Notes

  • Display only. It changes what the status bar shows, nothing else: the phone doesn't get service, and calls, SMS, emergency calling and cellular data are never touched.
  • With Podified, turn off Podified's Status Bar option, or it hides Mirage's bars.
