Crest

A music player for your own songs that plays them exactly as good as the files are.

Add to SileoAdd to Zebra

Add the repo, then search Crest.

  • Plays almost anything: FLAC, ALAC, WAV, MP3, AAC, Opus, DSD, CUE sheets and more.
  • Bit-Perfect mode: nothing changes the sound between the file and your headphones or DAC.
  • Gapless, crossfade and Automix, which blends songs on the beat.
  • EQ for each output: your speaker, AirPods, DAC and car each keep their own EQ, and Crest switches for you. AutoEQ import included.
  • Lyrics, smart playlists, scrobbling (Last.fm, ListenBrainz), CarPlay and a widget.

How it works

  • Every format through a decoder made for it: FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, Opus, Vorbis, APE, WavPack, Musepack, TAK, TTA, WMA, DSD (DSF and DFF) and CUE sheets.
  • Each song at its own sample rate. When the next song has a different rate (say 44.1 kHz, then 96 kHz), Crest switches the output rate instead of converting the song, with a very short pause only where the rates differ. Albums at one rate stay gapless.
  • It tells you when it can't. If your output can't play a file's rate, Crest resamples and shows a RESAMPLED tag. In Bit-Perfect mode it shows BIT-PERFECT, or why the song isn't.
  • Gapless, crossfade and Automix. Automix blends songs on the beat; while two songs blend, both play at one rate.
  • Loudness: ReplayGain (track or album) and EBU R128.
  • EQ for each output. Your speaker, AirPods, DAC and car each keep their own EQ, and Crest switches when the output changes. You can import AutoEQ curves.
  • Your Music app songs (jailbreak and TrollStore builds) are read where they already are. The first scan opens each song once to read its format and loudness; on a big library that takes a few minutes, so keep Crest open until it finishes.

Privacy

  • Offline by default. No analytics, no ads, no account. Crest only goes online for scrobbling or online lyrics, and both stay off until you turn them on.
  • Wi-Fi Transfer is locked. It only runs while its screen is open, and your computer needs the PIN shown on the phone.
  • Your files stay as they are. Songs from the Music app are read in place. Nothing is copied, converted or uploaded.

Which build to get

  • Jailbroken (Rootless or RootHide): this repo. Plays your Music app songs, has CarPlay, and adds music with Wi-Fi Transfer, Files or Filza.
  • TrollStore: the .tipa from GitHub. Music app songs and CarPlay; adds music with Wi-Fi Transfer.
  • Not jailbroken: the sideload .ipa from GitHub. No Music app songs or CarPlay (the sandbox blocks them); adds music with Wi-Fi Transfer.

What's New in 1.0.0

  • Out of beta. Thanks to everyone who tested and sent requests.
  • Every song at its own sample rate, one after another. When a song ended and the next one had a different rate (say 44.1 kHz, then 96 kHz), the next one was quietly converted to the old rate. Now Crest switches the output between them, with a very short pause only where the rates differ. Albums at one rate stay gapless. Crossfade and Automix still play both songs at one rate while they blend.
  • USB DACs that switch rates a moment late no longer get stuck converting everything to one rate.
  • Classic: turn the whole app into an iPod classic, with a click wheel you can really turn. Settings → Appearance → Player Skin. Browse by Playlists, Artists, Albums, Songs and Genres. The cover on the right now shows in full (it was cut in half).
  • CarPlay log: if CarPlay acts up, Settings → CarPlay → Share CarPlay Log and send it in an issue.
  • VU Meters now also shows greyed out in the Classic player, with the reason.

Works on

  • iOS 15 to 27. Rootless and RootHide jailbreaks.
  • Tested on an iPhone 7 (iOS 15.8.6), an iPhone 11 Pro Max (iOS 16.2), an iPhone XR (iOS 16.0.3), an iPhone SE 2nd gen (iOS 17.5.1) and an iPhone 11 Pro (iOS 18).
  • No jailbreak? The TrollStore and sideload versions are on GitHub. They add music with Wi-Fi Transfer only, for now.
  • If it ever sends your phone to Safe Mode, open Sileo (it still works there) and uninstall it. Then tell me.

Good to know

  • Add music with Library → Add from Files, or with Wi-Fi Transfer from a computer on the same network.
  • Apple Music subscription downloads can't play: Apple locks them to the Music app. Songs synced from a computer, bought on iTunes or uploaded to iCloud Music Library work.
  • CarPlay is built in but hasn't been tried in a real car yet. If you try it, tell me how it goes.
  • A song won't play or sounds wrong? Open an issue with your build (.deb, .tipa or .ipa), iOS version and the file's format (Crest shows it under Get Info).

Changelog3 earlier versions

Beta 4.1

  • Fixed: Beta 4 shipped with the plain fallback screens on iOS 15 to 17 (the newer look didn't load). 4.1 brings the normal screens back everywhere.
  • VU meters in the Minimal Player: the ⋯ menu now shows the VU Meters item greyed out with the reason (they only fit the Crest Player). It was missing on iOS 15.
  • Files import on sideloads: picking music in Files now shows the import and the result, and the picker accepts every audio type. Still needs testing on a real sideload.
  • Plus Beta 4: shuffle an artist from Search, no 50-song search limit, covers you add show right away.

Beta 4

  • Shuffle an artist: a shuffle button next to each artist in Search plays all their songs, from every album, in random order.
  • More search results: songs in Search no longer stop at 50.
  • Covers: a cover you add in Get Info now shows right away. Crest also finds folder art named cover, folder, front, album or AlbumArt (any case, jpg or png), and prefers a FLAC's front cover.

Thanks to HalfManHalfDragon on Discord for these.

Beta 3

  • Bit-Perfect mode: your songs reach the output exactly as they are in the file, with nothing in between. Tested sample for sample. A BIT-PERFECT label shows when it really is, and tapping the format chips on Now Playing tells you why when it isn't (Settings → Bit-Perfect).
  • Minimal Player: a calm, big-type layout for Now Playing, if you prefer it (Settings → Appearance).
  • Big libraries: you see the scan progress ("1,240 of 5,800 songs"), Crest saves as it goes, and it no longer closes during or after a long scan (thousands of songs in one album, or leaving the app mid-scan). Tested with 23,000 songs.
  • Fixed: the lyrics page could slide off the screen with no way out (with real cover art).
  • iOS 15 fixes: the Settings, Add and EQ Done buttons and the Deck icon were missing.
