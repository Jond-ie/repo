Crest

A high-quality offline music player for your own files. Every song plays at its own sample rate, and it looks like a piece of hi-fi gear, not an Apple Music clone.

  • Plays almost anything: FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, Opus, Vorbis, APE, WavPack, MPC, TAK, TTA, WMA, DSD and CUE sheets.
  • Bit-Perfect mode: nothing between the file and your DAC or headphones.
  • Gapless, crossfade and Automix: Automix blends songs on the beat, matches their loudness and keeps albums gapless.
  • 16-band parametric or graphic EQ with AutoEQ import. Each output keeps its own EQ (speaker, AirPods, USB DACs, CarPlay) and Crest switches when you plug in or connect.
  • Balance, stereo width, mono, reverb, tempo and key lock.
  • ReplayGain and EBU R128 loudness, synced lyrics, a visualizer, smart playlists, ratings and listening history.
  • Scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz, a Now Playing widget, CarPlay, Wi-Fi Transfer and Files import.
  • Plays the songs already synced to the Music app (not Apple Music downloads or protected songs).

What's New in 1.0.0~beta3

  • Bit-Perfect mode: your songs reach the output exactly as they are in the file, with nothing in between. Tested sample for sample. A BIT-PERFECT label shows when it really is, and tapping the format chips on Now Playing tells you why when it isn't (Settings → Bit-Perfect).
  • Minimal Player: a calm, big-type layout for Now Playing, if you prefer it (Settings → Appearance).
  • Big libraries: you see the scan progress ("1,240 of 5,800 songs"), Crest saves as it goes, and it no longer closes during or after a long scan (thousands of songs in one album, or leaving the app mid-scan). Tested with 23,000 songs.
  • Fixed: the lyrics page could slide off the screen with no way out (with real cover art).
  • iOS 15 fixes: the Settings, Add and EQ Done buttons and the Deck icon were missing.

Device Support

  • iOS 15 and up, rootless jailbreaks. Beta 3 tested on an iPhone 7 (A10) with iOS 15.8.6, an iPhone 11 Pro Max (A13) with iOS 16.2, and an iPhone SE (2nd gen, A13) with iOS 17.5.1. An earlier beta 3 build also ran on an iPhone 11 Pro (A13) with iOS 18.

Notes

  • Add music with Library → Add from Files or Wi-Fi Transfer (drag and drop from a computer on the same network).
  • No jailbreak? The TrollStore .tipa and sideload .ipa are on GitHub. Those builds add music with Wi-Fi Transfer only for now.
  • CarPlay hasn't been tested in a car yet: please report back if you try it.
  • This is a beta: please report songs that won't play or sound wrong.
