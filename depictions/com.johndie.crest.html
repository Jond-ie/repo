Crest

A high-quality offline music player for your own files. Every song plays at its own sample rate, and nothing in it looks like Apple Music.

  • Plays almost anything: FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, Opus, Vorbis, APE, WavPack, MPC, TAK, TTA, WMA, DSD and CUE sheets.
  • Gapless, crossfade and Automix: Automix blends songs on the beat, matches their loudness and keeps albums gapless.
  • 16-band parametric or graphic EQ with AutoEQ import. Each output keeps its own EQ (speaker, AirPods, USB DACs, CarPlay) and Crest switches when you plug in or connect.
  • Balance, stereo width, mono, reverb, tempo and key lock.
  • ReplayGain and EBU R128 loudness, synced lyrics, a visualizer, smart playlists, ratings and listening history.
  • Scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz, a Now Playing widget, CarPlay, Wi-Fi Transfer and Files import.
  • Plays the songs already synced to the Music app (not Apple Music downloads or protected songs).

What's New in 1.0.0~beta1

  • First public beta. Crest is free during the beta.

Device Support

  • iOS 15 and up, rootless. Tested on an iPhone 7 (A10) with iOS 15.8.6, an iPhone SE (2nd gen, A13) with iOS 17.5.1, an iPhone 11 Pro Max (A13) with iOS 16.2 and an iPhone 11 Pro (A13) with iOS 18.6.2.
  • Built for arm64 and arm64e.

Notes

  • Add music with Library → Add from Files or Wi-Fi Transfer (drag and drop from a computer on the same network).
  • CarPlay hasn't been tested in a car yet: please report back if you try it.
  • This is a beta: please report songs that won't play or sound wrong.
