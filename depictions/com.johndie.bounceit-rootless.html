Bounce It! (rootless)

Jake James's Bounce It! brought back for rootless jailbreaks: SpringBoard's springs get more mass and less damping, so animations overshoot and settle with a bounce: opening and closing apps, folders, swipes home and more.

  • Same spring values as the original Bounce It! 1.0.4, read from its binary and rebuilt for modern iOS.
  • No settings. Install, respring, done. To turn it off, uninstall it.

What's New in 1.0.4-1~jd2

  • The App Switcher stays steady: its cards no longer bounce and wander while you swipe through them or close apps. Opening and closing apps and the home swipe still bounce.

Device Support

  • iOS 15 – 18, rootless jailbreaks with ElleKit (Dopamine).
  • arm64 and arm64e (the current arm64e format, so it loads on A12 and newer; the 2019 original can't).
  • iOS 14 and newer moved the App Switcher and Reachability springs: those parts of the original only apply where iOS still has them. The main SpringBoard springs and fluid gestures bounce on every supported version.
  • Tested on an iPhone SE 2 (A13) with iOS 17.5.1 and an iPhone 11 Pro Max (A13) with iOS 16.2, Dopamine.

Notes

  • Replaces the original rootful package (com.jakeashacks.bounceit); they can't be installed together.
