BinaryPass

A binary passcode keypad: each Lock Screen passcode button shows its number as four dots instead of a digit.

  • Read the dots left to right as 8 4 2 1: a big dot counts, a small faint one doesn't. Add up the big ones.
  • 1 = small small small big, 5 = small big small big (4 + 1), 9 = big small small big (8 + 1), and 0 is four small dots.
  • The buttons, the passcode dots, Emergency and Cancel work exactly as before. Only the Lock Screen keypad changes.
  • No settings. Install, respring, done. To turn it off, uninstall it.

Device Support

  • arm64 and arm64e (A12 and newer) builds, made with Xcode's toolchain and checked for the correct arm64e format.
  • Tested on an iPhone 7 (A10) with iOS 15.8.6 and Dopamine. Also tested on an iPhone SE (2nd gen, A13) with iOS 17.5.1 (arm64e), so A12 and newer are confirmed. iOS 16 isn't confirmed yet: please report back. If it puts you into safe mode, open Sileo (safe mode still allows it) and uninstall it.

Notes

  • The keypad layout is the standard one (1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9 / 0), so muscle memory still works.
  • Can't be installed together with BlankPass (both change the same buttons).
  • Only for rootless jailbreaks with ElleKit (Dopamine).
