Glide

Slide to unlock is back. The shimmering slide to unlock from iOS 6 replaces "Press home to open" on the Lock Screen.

  • Slide right: the text follows your finger and fades, and letting go past a third of the way unlocks (or brings up the passcode).
  • A short slide springs back. The widgets page no longer opens by accident on the Lock Screen.
  • Home, Touch ID and your passcode work exactly as before. Notification Center isn't touched.
  • No settings.

What's New in beta2

  • Fixed: touches could stop working after sliding to unlock until a respring (most often on the first slide after a respring). If that happened to you on beta1, update.
  • Works with a passcode: sliding brings up the keypad, as a Home press does.
  • iOS 17 and A12 and newer confirmed (iPhone SE 2nd gen, iOS 17.5.1).
  • Sileo won't install it together with Pinstant (Pinstant skips the Lock Screen Glide lives on).

Device Support

  • arm64 and arm64e (A12 and newer) builds, made with Xcode's toolchain and checked for the correct arm64e format.
  • Tested on an iPhone 7 (A10) with iOS 15.8.6 and Dopamine. Also tested on an iPhone SE (2nd gen, A13) with iOS 17.5.1 (arm64e), so A12 and newer are confirmed. iOS 16 isn't confirmed yet: please report back. If it puts you into safe mode, open Sileo (safe mode still allows it) and uninstall it.

Notes

  • Beta. If something looks wrong, uninstall it and please report back.
  • Not compatible with tweaks that skip the Lock Screen (like Pinstant): there's nothing to slide.
  • Only for rootless jailbreaks with ElleKit (Dopamine).
