Podified

Makes a SIM-less iPhone present itself as an iPod. Every feature is a switch in Settings → Podified, all off by default:

  • Rename in Settings: only the word "iPhone" changes to "iPod" on About and Storage, and your own model stays (iPhone 13 Pro → iPod 13 Pro, iPhone Storage → iPod Storage). Your device name is left alone.
  • Hide Cellular: removes the Cellular row from Settings.
  • Status Bar: replaces "SOS only" / "No Service" with iPod, or hides it like an iPod touch, and removes the signal dots in Control Center.
  • Hide Phone App: hides Phone from the Home Screen, Dock and App Library (after a respring). The app stays installed.

Notes

  • Cosmetic only. Labels and visibility; emergency calling, SOS and the Phone app itself are never touched.
  • Built for SIM-less iPhones: with a SIM inserted, the carrier name is replaced too.
  • To keep Phone out of Spotlight, use Settings → Siri & Search → Phone → Show App in Search.
  • Tested on an iPhone 8 Plus (A11, arm64) with iOS 16.7 only. A12 and newer devices are untested; please report whether it works on yours.
