Density

More on screen in the apps you pick, like Android's display density setting. The app lays itself out for a bigger screen, so lists, feeds and pages fit more, while the status bar and keyboard stay exactly as they are.

  • Simple: Settings → Density. Set the Amount slider (0 is normal, 30 fits the most), then switch Density on for the apps you want.
  • Stock look: the keyboard and status bar are untouched, and text stays sharp.
  • Light: apps you don't switch on load nothing extra.
  • A change applies the next time the app opens: close it in the App Switcher first.

Device Support

  • arm64 and arm64e (A12 and newer) builds, made with Xcode's toolchain and checked for the correct arm64e format.
  • Tested on an iPhone 8 Plus (A11) with iOS 16.7 and Dopamine, in Settings, Notes, X, YouTube and Reddit, up to an Amount of 30. Reported working on an iPhone 15 Pro Max (A17 Pro) with iOS 17.3; more reports welcome: report back. If it puts you into safe mode, open Sileo (safe mode still allows it) and uninstall it.

Notes

  • Beta. Games aren't tested yet. If an app looks wrong, switch it off.
  • Needs AltList and PreferenceLoader (Sileo installs them).
  • Only for rootless jailbreaks (Dopamine).
