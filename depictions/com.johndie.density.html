Density

More on screen in the apps you pick, like Android's display density setting. The app lays itself out for a bigger screen, so lists, feeds and pages fit more, while the status bar and keyboard stay exactly as they are.

  • Simple: Settings → Density. Set the Amount slider (0 is normal, 30 fits the most), then switch Density on for the apps you want.
  • Stock look: the keyboard and status bar are untouched, and text stays sharp.
  • Light: apps you don't switch on load nothing extra.
  • Changed something? Tap Apply Changes: it closes the apps you changed and resprings.

What's New in 1.0.0

  • Out of beta, for iOS 15–18.
  • Face ID iPhones: chat bars and text boxes no longer tuck under the keyboard in any app (Reddit, Swiftgram and others). The strip below the keys (globe, dictation) is now counted at the right size everywhere.
  • Rotation and split screen: apps fill their space when they rotate or when a split-screen tweak resizes them, instead of shrinking.
  • Since the betas: chat boxes stay above the keyboard in Messages, Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Reddit and more, on iOS 15, 16, 17 and 18.

Notes

  • Rootless jailbreaks (Dopamine), iOS 15–18, arm64 and arm64e.
  • Needs AltList and PreferenceLoader (Sileo installs them).
  • If an app looks wrong, switch Density off for it and please report it. Games aren't tested.
