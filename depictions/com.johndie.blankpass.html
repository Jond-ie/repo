BBlankPass

Hides the numbers on your passcode keypad.

Add to SileoAdd to Zebra

Add the repo, then search BlankPass.

The numbers and letters on the Lock Screen keypad are hidden, so only the circles show.

  • Everything else works as before: the dots, Emergency and Cancel.
  • Pressing a key doesn't show its number either.
  • Only the Lock Screen keypad changes. The keypads in Phone and Settings stay normal.
  • No settings. To turn it off, uninstall it.
Version
1.0.0
Jailbreak
Rootless, RootHide

Works on

  • iOS 15 to 18. Rootless and RootHide jailbreaks.
  • Tested on an iPhone 7 (iOS 15.8.6) and an iPhone SE 2nd gen (iOS 17.5.1).
  • If it ever sends your phone to Safe Mode, open Sileo (it still works there) and uninstall it. Then tell me.

Good to know

  • Know your passcode by heart. The keys are in the usual order: 1 2 3, 4 5 6, 7 8 9, 0.
