BegoneCIA

iOS 15–27

One Control Center button that cuts off the camera, microphone and location for every app. Apps don't get an error, they just get nothing: silence, no picture, no location.

  • Turns itself off during calls (on by default), so people can still hear you.
  • Pick apps where it stays off while they're open.
  • Add the button in Settings → Control Center.
Version
1.2.0
Jailbreak

What's New in 1.2.0

  • iOS 27: now works on iOS 15 through 27.
  • Nepeta's original Settings page is back: Enabled, Disable in calls and Disable BegoneCIA for applications, laid out like the original.
  • Disable in calls: on by default. While you're on a call, BegoneCIA steps aside so the other person can hear you, then turns back on when you hang up. Tested with phone calls; FaceTime and apps like WhatsApp use the same system and should work too, so tell me if they don't.
  • Disable for applications: pick apps where BegoneCIA switches off while they're open.
  • Fixes: location could still reach some apps on iOS 15, and A12 and newer phones on iOS 15.0 and 15.1 could crash.

Works on

  • iOS 15 to 27. Rootless and RootHide jailbreaks.
  • Needs CCSupport, Cephei (from the Chariz repo), PreferenceLoader and AltList.
  • On iOS 27, open its settings from the TweakSettings app.
  • If it ever sends your phone to Safe Mode, open Sileo (it still works there) and uninstall it. Then tell me.

Good to know

  • A jailbroken phone is less secure overall. BegoneCIA protects your privacy, but it doesn't change that.
  • Set it up in 1.1.0? Your app list keeps working.
