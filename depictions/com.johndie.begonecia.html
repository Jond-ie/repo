Begonecia

A Control Center toggle that silences the microphone, camera and location across the whole system. When it's on, apps don't get a permission error — they just get nothing: zeroed mic audio, no camera frames, no location callbacks. A silent blackout, not a visible denial.

Toggle it from Control Center; add the tile in Settings → Control Center.

Device Support

A12 and newer are supported again, but not confirmed yet. Earlier builds put A12+ devices (iPhone XS / XR and later) into safe mode because their A12+ part was built in the wrong format. This version is built with a new toolchain and passes the format check, but it hasn't been confirmed on a real A12+ device yet.

  • On A12+? Please try it and report back, whether it works or not.
  • If it puts you into safe mode: open Sileo (safe mode still allows it) and uninstall it.
  • Tested on an iPhone 8 Plus (A11), iOS 16.7.

Notes

  • The mic silencer targets the common capture path; verify on your setup.
  • Jailbreaking lowers your device's baseline security — this hardens privacy, it doesn't undo that.
  • Requires CCSupport (com.opa334.ccsupport), Cephei (ws.hbang.common, from Chariz), and ellekit (ships with Dopamine).
  • Tested on an iPhone 8 Plus (A11, arm64) with iOS 16.7. A12 and newer: see Device Support.
