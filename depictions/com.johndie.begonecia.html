Begonecia

A Control Center toggle that silences the microphone, camera and location across the whole system. When it's on, apps don't get a permission error — they just get nothing: zeroed mic audio, no camera frames, no location callbacks. A silent blackout, not a visible denial.

Toggle it from Control Center; add the tile in Settings → Control Center.

What's New in 1.1.0

  • Per-app control: new Settings page. Block everywhere, everywhere except the apps you pick, or only in the apps you pick.
  • Triggr actions: with Triggr 2.0, Turn BegoneCIA On / Off / Toggle are Triggr actions (any button, gesture or event).
  • Fixes: Wallet / Apple Pay crash on iOS 18, a parsecd crash, and a Snapchat crash when turning BegoneCIA off.
  • Now also needs PreferenceLoader and AltList (installed automatically).

Notes

  • Rootless jailbreaks with ElleKit (Dopamine), iOS 15–18, arm64 and arm64e.
  • Requires CCSupport, Cephei (ws.hbang.common, from Chariz), PreferenceLoader and AltList.
  • Jailbreaking lowers your device's baseline security. Begonecia hardens privacy; it doesn't undo that.
  • If it ever puts you into safe mode, open Sileo and uninstall it.
