With edge protect on, the first swipe up only shows the Home Bar; swipe again to go home. A port of XEdgeProtect by smokin1337.

  • Pick where: all apps, only the apps you choose, or while the keyboard is up. A second list turns it off for chosen apps.
  • Auto-Hide Home Bar: hides the bar shortly after you open an app, like iOS 26 and 27, with a slider for the delay.
  • Floating dock tweaks: stop the swipe-up dock gesture in all apps or in chosen apps.
  • Everything is off until you turn it on in Settings → XEdgeProtect.
Version
1.0.0
Jailbreak
Rootless, RootHide

What's New in 1.0.0

  • First release of the port. It keeps the original's options and settings keys.
  • Changed from the original: the old faint Home Bar is gone, so the bar is either shown normally or hidden; the conflict pop-ups are replaced by short notes under each group.
  • Added: Auto-Hide timing that matches iOS 26 and 27.

Works on

  • iOS 15 to 27. Rootless and RootHide jailbreaks.
  • Tested by hand on an iPhone XR (iOS 16.0.3).
  • Not run on iOS 15, 17, 18, 26 or 27. Every method it uses was checked against those versions' system files, but it hasn't been run there. RootHide is untested too.
  • If it ever sends your phone to Safe Mode, open Sileo (it still works there) and uninstall it. Then tell me.

Good to know

  • It replaces the original XEdgeProtect. The two can't be installed together.
  • Needs AltList for the app lists.
  • The "Disabled" lists win over everything else, even when an app asks for edge protect itself.

Credits
