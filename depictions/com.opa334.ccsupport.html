CCSupport (iOS 18 fix)

CCSupport by opa334, the tweak that loads Control Center modules from other tweaks, with one fix for iOS 18.

  • The problem: on iOS 18, whenever SpringBoard runs without tweaks (safe mode, or after a reboot before you re-jailbreak), iOS removes every tweak's module from Control Center, so you have to add them back each time.
  • The fix: with CCSupport running, the Control Center layout is kept in its own file, so those modules survive safe mode and reboots. Add your modules back once after installing.
  • Everything else is CCSupport as you know it.

Recommended for

  • iOS 18 with rootless Dopamine. It only installs on iOS 18 and newer; iOS 15–17 should keep the official CCSupport.
  • Not tested on iOS 26.
  • When opa334 releases an official update, it replaces this one automatically.
