EQELinker

iOS 15–27

Control EQE with Triggr. One preset when your AirPods connect, another when they disconnect, or any preset on any trigger.

  • Adds an EQE Presets list to Triggr's actions, with the presets you saved in EQE.
  • Adds EQE Effects: turn the compressor or crossfeed on, off, or toggle them.
  • For EQ per device, use Bluetooth Device Connected / Disconnected as the trigger.
  • No settings.
Version
1.1.0
Jailbreak
Rootless, RootHide

Get it from the pwnapple repo: add it to your package manager, then search for EQELinker.

What's New in 1.1.0

  • EQE Effects: new Triggr actions that turn EQE's compressor or crossfeed on, off, or toggle them. For example: a preset and the compressor when your AirPods connect.

Works on

  • iOS 15 to 27. Rootless and RootHide jailbreaks.
  • Needs Triggr and EQE. EQE is installed separately.
  • If it ever sends your phone to Safe Mode, open Sileo (it still works there) and uninstall it. Then tell me.
