EQELinker

Load your saved EQE presets from Triggr: one preset when your AirPods connect, another when they disconnect, or any preset on any trigger.

  • Adds an EQE Presets category to Triggr's actions, listing the presets you saved in EQE.
  • Pair it with Custom Events → Bluetooth Device Connected / Disconnected for per-device EQ.
  • Talks to EQE only through EQE's own preset commands.
  • No settings.

Requirements

  • Triggr 1.0.4 or newer and EQE (installed separately).
  • Rootless jailbreaks (Dopamine), iOS 15–18.
