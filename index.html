John's Repo

Rootless privacy & utility tweaks for Dopamine (iOS 15–17, A12 and newer supported).

Add to Sileo

Or add https://jond-ie.github.io/repo/ manually in Sileo → Sources.

A12 and newer: supported and confirmed. Every tweak is tested on an iPhone SE (2nd gen, A13) with iOS 17.5.1, alongside iOS 16.7 (A11) and iOS 15.8.6 (A10) (Crossfade and Scrub: installed and loading, features not re-checked on iOS 17). If anything ever puts you into safe mode, uninstall it in Sileo and please report back.

Tweaks

Begonecia port

Control Center toggle that silences camera, microphone and location system-wide — total blackout, no permission errors. Rootless port of Nepeta's BegoneCIA (MIT).

Original BegoneCIA (mirror) · Source code

Slaplock

Gate any app behind the stock Face ID / Touch ID prompt. Per-app, all off by default.

Triggr

Activator-lite: assign actions to the home, lock and volume buttons, Touch ID, status bar taps, Home Screen icon flicks, shaking and events. Open source (GPL-3.0). Works on A12 and newer (tested on iOS 17.5.1).

Details · Source & feedback on GitHub

Pinstant

Wake straight to the passcode: no Lock Screen, no swipe, no wait. No settings. Concept from UnlockOrElse by Ethan Whited (ETHN).

Details

BlankPass

A blank Lock Screen passcode keypad: the numbers are hidden, the circles stay. No settings.

Details

BinaryPass

A binary Lock Screen passcode keypad: each number is shown as four dots (8 4 2 1). No settings. Concept from BinaryPasscode by eskimo (Jordan Koch).

Details

Quintet beta

Five columns of icons on the Home Screen, widgets resized to fit, and a five-icon Dock (which can be switched off in Settings).

Details

Glide beta

Slide to unlock is back: iOS 6's shimmering prompt on the Lock Screen, and a slide to the right unlocks. No settings.

Details

Shelf beta

The iOS 6 glass dock: your Dock icons on a glass shelf with reflections, drawn in code. No settings.

Details

Density beta

More on screen in the apps you pick, like Android's display density. The status bar and keyboard stay stock; one slider, then switch it on per app.

Details

Podified

Makes a SIM-less iPhone present itself as an iPod: iPod naming in Settings, no Cellular, an "iPod" status bar and an optional hidden Phone app. Cosmetic only.

Crossfade

True crossfade between local-library tracks in the stock Music app. For your own files — no subscription needed.

Scrub

Removes GPS & identifying metadata from photos before apps send them. Preserves orientation and color.

Heads-up: jailbreaking itself lowers your device's baseline security. These tweaks harden your privacy (what apps see and send); they don't undo that tradeoff. Install what you need, nothing more.
